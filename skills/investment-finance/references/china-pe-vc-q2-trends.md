## 1. New LPA/SPA Trends Under the New Company Law

### Key Clause-Level Developments (Q2 2026)

**Capital Contribution Period (Art. 47, 228):** Mandatory 5-year contribution period for LLCs. SPAs now include milestone-based drawdown schedules tied to this. Transferee joint liability for transferor's unpaid capital (Art. 88) — SPAs mandate transferor warranties on capital contribution status with indemnity provisions.

**Board/Audit Committee Reform:** Supervisor optional for small LLCs (Art. 83). Audit committees can replace supervisors. LPA drag-along/tag-along provisions now reference audit committee approvals instead of supervisor approvals.

**Preemptive Rights Modification (Art. 84):** Preemptive rights can be waived or modified in AoA. SPAs now include explicit preemptive right waiver/opt-out language.

**Derivative Actions Expanded (Art. 189):** LPA governance provisions add carve-outs for shareholder derivative suits. GP liability insurance provisions updated to cover derivative action defense costs.

**ESOP/Buyback Simplification (Art. 89, 162):** SPA reps & warranties now cover ESOP compliance with simplified repurchase rules.

### Notable Q2 2026 LPA Trends
1. **"Sunset Clause" Adoption**: ~67% of new China-focused LPAs include 10-year hard cap (up from ~40% in 2023).
2. **Key Person Provisions**: Enhanced to include regulatory compliance triggers.
3. **MFN Clauses**: Increasingly granular — not just management fees but side-letter economic terms.
4. **Distressed GP Provisions**: LPAC can suspend management fees if NAV drops below 70% of committed capital for 2 consecutive quarters (~30% of new LPAs).
5. **Waterfall Distribution Changes**: American waterfall giving way to European waterfall in RMB funds.

## 2. AI / Data Compliance Clauses in Term Sheets

### Standard AI/Data Clauses (Q2 2026)

**(a) AI Compliance Representation & Warranty Block**
Target represents all AI/ML models:
- Comply with PRC AI Law and MIIT/CAC filing requirements
- Undergo required safety assessments for generative AI
- Do not use training data violating PIPL
- Maintain required algorithm registration certificates
- **Q2 2026 update**: Includes **model card disclosure** — training data provenance documentation

**(b) Data Schedule Checklist**
All PI categories processed, retention periods, cross-border data flows listed.

**(c) AI-Specific MAC Clause**
MAC includes: (i) regulatory action restricting AI services; (ii) CAC order to cease genAI operations; (iii) material amendment to AI classification rules.

**(d) Algorithm Audit Rights (Q2 2026 Emerging Practice)**
Investors requesting independent algorithm audit rights pre-closing where AI >30% of revenue. Scope: bias, data provenance, regulatory filing status, IP ownership of model weights.

**(e) Post-Closing Covenants**
- Notify investor of CAC/MIIT investigation within 5 business days
- Right to forced divestment/put if core AI product banned or classified as "unacceptable risk"

## 4. S-Fund / Secondary Market Transaction Trends

### Market Overview (Q2 2026)
- China secondary volume: ~RMB 350-500B projected (2026), up from ~RMB 200B (2024)
- GP-led discounts: 15-25% (improved from 25-35% in 2023)
- LP-led discounts: 5-15% for quality assets

### Key Structural Developments
**(a) Government-Backed S-Platforms**
- Shanghai S-Fund Exchange: ~RMB 50B quarterly volume; launched standardized S-Product templates (Q2 2026)
- LGFG-backed S-funds: 35% of secondary capital

**(b) GP-Led Secondary Trends**
- Stapled secondaries (new capital + continuation fund) becoming standard
- Single-asset continuation funds gaining traction for AI/biotech trophy assets
- **Q2 2026 innovation**: "Asset-backed secondary" — continuation fund backed by tranched co-investment rights

**(c) LPA Provisions Evolution**
| Clause | 2023 Practice | Q2 2026 |

| Transfer Restriction | GP consent; LPAC approval for >50% | Tiered: LPAC only if buyer not QII |

| GP-led Disclosure | Limited | Enhanced 2-page GP conflict economics summary |

**(d) Regulatory Updates**
